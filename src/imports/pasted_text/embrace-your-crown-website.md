Embrace Your Crown
Creating a polished salon website for a beauty business rooted in confidence, care, and self-expression

Embrace Your Crown is a beauty salon located at The AUX in Evanston. The salon offers hair, beauty, lash, brow, and beard care services, with a focus on helping clients refresh their style, express themselves, and “own their royalty.”

Tapestri supported Embrace Your Crown with a WordPress website that helped translate the salon’s personality, services, and visual identity into a clear, welcoming digital presence. The project included client discovery, design exploration, content organization, service-page development, gallery support, and launch preparation.

The Challenge

Embrace Your Crown needed a website that could help new and returning clients quickly understand what the salon offers, learn about the owner, view service categories, browse photos, and get in touch or book an appointment.

Because this was a small business with a strong personal brand, the website needed to feel polished without losing the warmth and individuality of the salon. The client had ideas about colors, personality, and design elements, but also needed guidance in figuring out what belonged on the site and how the information should be organized. The team’s discovery process included reviewing other salon and beauty websites, identifying common features, interviewing the client, and using that conversation to clarify content and design direction.

Key needs included:

a clear homepage that introduced the salon’s personality and services
organized service categories with descriptions and pricing
an “About Tiffany” page centered on the owner’s story and expertise
a gallery to showcase hair and beauty work
testimonials to build trust with prospective clients
contact and booking pathways
visual styling that reflected the salon’s brand and energy
a WordPress site that could be edited and maintained over time
Our Approach

The project followed the same real-world software development lifecycle used across the client work: discovery, design, development, testing, release, and maintenance planning. The process was iterative, with the team researching, prototyping, sharing work, gathering feedback, and revising the site based on the client’s needs.

During discovery, the team reviewed other salon and beauty websites to identify what information clients expect to find, including services, pricing, photos, testimonials, owner information, and contact details. The team also interviewed the client to better understand the salon’s personality, preferred colors, visual style, and business goals.

The design process began with low-fidelity planning in Google Slides, where the team explored page structure, messaging, and layout ideas. The team then moved into higher-fidelity design and implementation using Figma and WordPress tools including Elementor and related widget libraries.

What We Built
A welcoming homepage

The homepage introduces Embrace Your Crown with a strong, memorable message: “Your Crown is HERE!” It positions the salon as an all-in-one destination for hair, beauty, and beard care, and gives visitors a quick sense of the salon’s services and personality.

The page was designed to help prospective clients immediately understand what the business offers and feel invited into the salon’s brand.

Organized service categories

The site organizes services into client-friendly categories such as Natural Vibes, Care for the Crown, Relax it Out, and Protective. These categories help visitors scan the options and find the kind of service they need without reading through one long, overwhelming list.

The services page includes detailed offerings and prices for treatments such as silk press, roller set silk out, two-strand twists, twist updos, flat twists, Bantu knots, rod sets, and keratin express treatments.

A stronger personal brand

Because the salon is closely tied to its owner’s identity and expertise, the site includes an About Tiffany section. This gave the business a more personal, trust-building presence and helped visitors connect the salon experience to the person behind it.

The team had initially considered a broader “Meet the Team” structure, but client feedback clarified that the site needed to center Tiffany instead. The team adjusted the site structure accordingly, replacing the team concept with a more appropriate owner-focused page.

Gallery and visual storytelling

For a beauty salon, photos are essential. The site includes a hair gallery with the heading “Let the Crown be Placed,” giving prospective clients a visual sense of the salon’s work and style.

The team also had to be thoughtful about sourcing visual material, including finding appropriate non-copyrighted photos and videos where needed.

WordPress implementation

The team built the site in WordPress using templates, widgets, and page-building tools. This allowed the team to create a more polished site while still using a platform that could be maintained over time.

The project included creating pages, organizing content, simplifying text for web readers, preparing a prototype for client feedback, and revising the site based on that feedback.

The Result

The final website gives Embrace Your Crown a clear and professional digital home. Visitors can quickly understand the salon’s services, browse categories and pricing, view a gallery, read testimonials, learn about Tiffany, and find ways to connect or book.

The site reflects the salon’s confidence-centered brand while making practical information easier to find. It also gives the business a stronger foundation for visibility, credibility, and client communication.
