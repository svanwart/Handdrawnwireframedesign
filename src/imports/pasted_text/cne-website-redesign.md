Childcare Network of Evanston
Helping a community-centered nonprofit redesign its website for clarity, accessibility, and easier content management

Childcare Network of Evanston needed a refreshed website that better reflected its mission: connecting young children and families with high-quality, equitable early childhood education, resources, and community support.

The existing site contained valuable information, but the organization needed a clearer structure, a more modern and mobile-friendly design, and a simpler way for staff to update content over time. Tapestri supported the redesign and Webflow implementation, helping CNE create a site that is easier for families, educators, supporters, and staff to use.

The Challenge

CNE serves several audiences at once: families looking for early childhood programs and resources, educators seeking professional development, community members interested in events and giving opportunities, and staff who need to keep the website current.

The redesign needed to make information easier to find while also giving CNE staff more control over day-to-day updates. Key needs included:

clearer navigation organized around major audience groups
mobile-friendly, responsive page layouts
improved accessibility and usability
reusable templates and components for consistent design
a content management structure that made updates easier for staff
a more welcoming experience for families new to CNE’s services
Our Approach

We began by analyzing the existing website, stakeholder needs, content structure, and platform options. The team reviewed how different audiences might arrive at the site and what they would need to accomplish once there.

This work informed a new information architecture organized around CNE’s primary audiences, including families, educators, supporters, and community partners. We also helped evaluate website platforms and selected Webflow because it offered a strong balance of design flexibility, CMS functionality, and ease of editing for nontechnical content managers.

From there, the team developed responsive Webflow templates, reusable design components, CMS collections, and structured content areas that could grow with the organization.

What We Built
A clearer path for families

We created a step-by-step guide for new families to help explain how to access CNE’s services. This page translated a complex process into a more approachable sequence, with links to relevant information and next steps.

The goal was to help families understand where to begin, what support was available, and how to move forward without needing to search across multiple disconnected pages.

A more flexible CMS structure

We helped move key content into Webflow’s CMS so staff could update important information without rebuilding pages manually.

One example was the program partners section. Instead of maintaining partner information as static page content, the redesigned site used CMS entries and dynamic templates. This made it easier to add logos, links, categories, and partner details while keeping the page visually consistent.

Reusable Webflow templates and components

The redesign used reusable components and structured design patterns to help maintain consistency across the site. This included responsive layouts, content cards, dynamic templates, and design-system-based styling.

These choices helped make the site easier to maintain and reduced the risk of pages drifting visually over time as new content was added.

Support for launch, accessibility, and maintainability

In addition to implementation, the project included attention to responsive behavior, accessibility considerations, content organization, form testing, CMS data entry, and staff editing workflows.

The goal was not only to launch a better website, but to leave CNE with a site they could continue using and updating confidently.

The Result

The redesigned website gave CNE a clearer, more modern, and more maintainable web presence.

The new structure helps visitors find information based on who they are and what they need: families, educators, supporters, or community members. The Webflow CMS gives staff a more intuitive way to manage content, update partner information, and maintain a consistent look and feel across the site.

While evaluation and user testing were still ongoing in the draft materials, the client was pleased with the improved organization, visual consistency, and ease of editing.
